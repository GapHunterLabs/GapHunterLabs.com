Dependency and CI/CD security review

Security

I do a read-only review of the repositories you choose: known vulnerabilities in production dependencies, dependency-confusion risks in manifests and lockfiles, and CI/CD workflow permissions, checked with OSV-Scanner and OpenSSF Scorecard. I can add an EU Cyber Resilience Act readiness check: security policy, security.txt, SBOM and the vulnerability-reporting workflow (technical, not legal advice). My written report gives each finding with its source and the version that fixes it, when one exists. If anything is exploitable, I report it to you first, through your security channel.
